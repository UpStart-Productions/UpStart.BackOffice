Iteration label text: "2A  Folder Cut".
Retain the navy, teal and manila gold family of the reference, on a very pale cool ivory background; refine the original folder idea radically. Build a SINGLE compact, broad tabbed manila folder silhouette with a shallow flat file tab raised on its upper left. Inside the lower portion, carve ONE strong continuous geometric negative-space cut: two vertical upright spaces joined by a deliberate central downward V so the remaining structure reads unmistakably as a capital M. Think a capital M cleverly discovered in a real folder, with the folder contour doing useful structural work. The main folder must be warm manila gold, with at most one offset narrow teal rear folder edge for depth. No three-folder rainbow stack, no rounded two-door arches, no pasted letter on a rectangle, no printed M. Use a restrained refined navy humanist sans wordmark, medium-bold, with clean adult proportions, subtly softened corners, a double-storey a and excellent kerning. Icon must be a meaningful original folder-M fusion, readable in its single-color version with no reliance on the accent color.
