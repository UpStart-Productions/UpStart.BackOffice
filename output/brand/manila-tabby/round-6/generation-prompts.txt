Handwriting direction: TIDY FELT-TIP PRINT. Use original natural hand-printed letters, medium-bold rounded marker strokes, mostly monoline, separate letters with slight organic variations in letter height and baseline. A relaxed office folder label written neatly with a fine felt-tip marker, with warmth and personality. Clearly handwritten rather than a rounded typeset font. Upright or only a very mild right slant. Smooth crisp ink silhouette, no dry-brush grain, no messy jitter, no Comic Sans, no bubbly cartoon script. Natural spacing, no contrived swashes. Keep the name balanced and substantial without filling every inch of the tab.
