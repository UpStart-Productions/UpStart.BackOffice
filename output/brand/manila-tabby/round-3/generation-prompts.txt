04a-side-tab
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a NEW refined Manila product logo concept board. Manila is independent back-office software for agencies and small teams: clients, projects, time tracking and invoices. User feedback: our previous M assembled from folded folder panels looked too close to the Gmail logo. Make a decisive visual departure. NO M-shaped standalone icon, NO envelope, NO chevron, NO central V fold, NO multicolored ribbon, NO four-color Google-like palette. A normal typographic capital M in the word "Manila" is of course required; leave it a normal letter. No cat or mascot: the approved Tabby cat remains unchanged and is not part of this generation.
Palette: deep aubergine ink, warm manila paper gold, warm pale ivory, restrained coral allowed. Flat vector-like graphic design with precise edges and purposeful negative space, minimal and mature, appealing next to a friendly illustrated orange tabby cat. No gradients, shadows, grain, embossing, 3D or photography.
Wide landscape concept board, 1536x1024-style. Small unobtrusive iteration label upper left. Large primary horizontal logo lockup occupying the upper-middle. Lower row shows a monochrome version of the exact same identity, a white reversed version on aubergine, and a rounded-square app-icon application of the same motif. These are scale/color tests of ONE design, not new symbols. Preserve same geometry in every application. Generous whitespace, clean margins, no divider lines, no slogans, no explanatory prose. All text must be exact: "Manila" and the specified iteration label only. Original restrained design with enough distinctive optical detail to feel like a brand rather than a stock icon.
Iteration label: "4A  Side Tab".
Design a FOLDER-FIRST symbol: a compact front-facing upright manila folder, a broad but not wide solid paper pocket, with a single oversized flat file tab offset on its LEFT SIDE near the top. The tab projects laterally left, making an unmistakably asymmetric silhouette. A thin second aubergine or cream paper profile sits slightly behind the pocket to the right. One short horizontal negative-space slot near the upper edge expresses the opening of a folder; all structural geometry is vertical, horizontal, or gently rounded. NO diagonal lines anywhere in the symbol, NO letter hidden in it, NO rectangular envelope with triangular flap. The design should feel like a physical index folder turned upright, with the eccentric side tab creating recognition. Main icon in manila gold with aubergine narrow rear profile. Wordmark Manila is aubergine, carefully kerned, confident humanist sans with subtly rounded corners, moderate weight, readable double-storey a. The icon must stand on its asymmetric silhouette in single color. Do not add text, stripes, checkmarks or dots to the folder. The app icon uses this same side-tab folder silhouette, no letters.

Iteration label: "4B  Wordmark Tab".
Make Manila a WORDMARK-LED identity with no standalone symbol beside the name. A beautifully drawn confident aubergine humanist sans wordmark "Manila", subtly rounded corners, professionally restrained, strong but not heavy, balanced open counters and excellent spacing. Ordinary recognizable capital M with no modification. The ONLY brand twist is the dot of the lowercase i: replace that dot with a tiny manila-gold index-tab shape, a horizontal rounded rectangular paper tab with a very small stepped upper-left shoulder, floating clearly above the i stem like a normal dot. Keep it compact and legible, not an illustration sitting on the text. This warm paper-tab detail supplies the folder association without forcing a pictogram or monogram. The gold tab motif may be enlarged and used alone in the app icon on aubergine, precisely the same contour as the dot above i. Show the full wordmark in its mono and reversed tests in the lower row, plus this tab-only app icon. Do not introduce any separate folder icon, M icon, letter tile, underline, tab around the whole word, envelope, diagonal panel or cat. The main wordmark should dominate the composition, beautifully proportioned and unmistakably spell Manila.
