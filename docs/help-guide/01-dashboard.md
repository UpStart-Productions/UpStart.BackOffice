# Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** from the left nav (home icon). It is the landing page after sign-in: how the business is doing and what needs you today. You only see the cards your role can use.

The header greets you by name, shows today's date, and sums up what needs you: overdue items, things due today, invoices ready to send and work not yet billed.

## Mine / Everyone's

The **Mine / Everyone's** switch at the top right changes the Activity lists (next steps, gone quiet, unfiled email), the invoice figures and unbilled work. **Mine** shows your items, the invoices you created and your own unbilled time; **Everyone's** shows the whole team's. **My work** and **My time** always show your own.

## Cards

Each card has an icon and title in its header, a grip handle for moving it, and a **→** link to the full page. Rows show the record on the left (title and one line of detail, both cut short with … when long) and a date tag or button on the right. Click a row's title to open it.

- **Financial health** (roles with Invoices): pick a date range at the top of the card (**This month**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Year to date**, **Last 12 months**; remembered on this browser). Everything on the card uses it. Four figures: **Collected** (invoices paid in the range and how many, compared with the same length of time just before it), **Outstanding** (invoices sent in the range and not paid yet, with the overdue amount), **Unbilled work** (billable time and expenses from the range not on an invoice yet), and **Net** (income minus expenses from Accounting, admins only; others see **Drafts to send**). Below them, **Invoiced vs collected** charts the range (by week for This month, by month otherwise), and **Needs action** lists invoice drafts to send (**Review & send**), overdue invoices (**Open**) and clients with unbilled work (**Invoice** opens a new invoice for that client with all its unbilled time and expenses already added as lines, from the earliest one through today; nothing is saved until you review it and click **Save**). Click an unbilled row's name to see what makes up the number: each project's time entries with date, person, notes, hours, rate (and whether it came from the entry, the project or the person) and amount, plus unbilled expenses and the account's last invoice. Tags flag time with **No project**, a **Project not billable**, and hours dated **before last invoice** (probably billed already but never linked to an invoice). **Create invoice** and **Open account** are at the bottom. **→** opens Reports.
- **Today**: the icon in the card's header is a small sky showing today's weather (sunny, cloudy, rain, snow, night and so on) for the business address in **Settings**. Click it and the sky opens across the header with the temperature, conditions, high and low, and chance of rain or snow; click again to go back. The pin button there switches to where you are (your browser asks once). Below, everything due today in one list. **Overdue** first (tasks past their due date and next steps that are late; the three oldest show, then a line counting the rest with links to **Tasks** and **Next steps**), then **Today**: bookings and your shifts today, tasks and next steps due today, form submissions waiting for review, and email to file. The check button marks a task or an account's next step done. Next steps more than 30 days late collapse into one line at the bottom; **Clean up** opens them in **Activity → To do → Next steps**.
- **My work** (roles with Tasks): tasks assigned to you. The check button on a row marks the task done. **Overdue** shows the three oldest overdue tasks and a line counting the rest (**All tasks** opens them). **New for you** lists tasks someone else gave you and tasks with a new comment since your last visit. **Due this week** lists the rest due today through the next seven days. Each row shows who gave you the task (their avatar), the project, subtasks done and the latest comment. The line at the bottom counts tasks due later or with no date; **All tasks** opens Tasks.
- **Pipeline & relationships**: open cards in your first pipeline, the total of their **Estimated value**, and each lane with its card count and a bar showing its share of the open cards. Click a lane to open Pipeline. **Gone quiet** lists active accounts nobody has contacted in a while, with days since last contact. **Next steps this week** lists next steps coming up. **→** opens Pipeline.
- **Since last time**: what happened in the last week, newest first: payments received, comments on your tasks, tasks given to you, bookings made, form submissions and email that came in unfiled. Anything after your last visit is tagged **New**. **→** opens Activity.
- **My time** (roles with Time): hours this week and the share that is billable, with **Log time** (or the running timer and its stop button). **By project** shows your four biggest projects this week with their hours; click one to open it. A bar for each day Monday to Sunday sits at the bottom of the card.
- **My shifts** (Care module, roles with Schedule): your shifts for the next week. **Start visit** on today's shifts starts the visit for that shift; **Open visit** once it's started. **→** opens the Schedule.

## Rearranging

Drag a card by the grip handle in its header to move it. Each card has a natural width (Financial health two-thirds, Today and My work half, the rest a third). Cards fill rows in your order, and the last card in a row stretches to fill the space left, so there are never gaps; two thirds side by side become a third and a two-thirds. Your order is remembered on this computer and browser.
