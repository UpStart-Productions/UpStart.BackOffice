# Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** from the left nav (home icon). It is the landing page after sign-in: how the business is doing and what needs you today. You only see the cards your role can use.

The header greets you by name, shows today's date, and sums up what needs you: overdue items, things due today, invoices ready to send and work not yet billed.

## Mine / Everyone's

The **Mine / Everyone's** switch at the top right changes the Activity lists (next steps, gone quiet, unfiled email), the invoice figures and unbilled work. **Mine** shows your items, the invoices you created and your own unbilled time; **Everyone's** shows the whole team's. **My work** and **My time** always show your own.

## Cards

Each card has an icon and title in its header, a **⋯** handle for moving it, and a **→** link to the full page. Rows show the record on the left (title and one line of detail, both cut short with … when long) and a date tag or button on the right. Click a row's title to open it.

- **Financial health** (roles with Invoices): four figures for this month: **Collected** (paid invoices, compared with last month up to the same day), **Outstanding** (sent and unpaid, with the overdue amount), **Unbilled work** (finished billable time and billable expenses not on an invoice yet), and **Net this month** (income minus expenses from Accounting, admins only; others see **Drafts to send**). Below them, **Invoiced vs collected** charts the last six months, and **Needs action** lists invoice drafts to send (**Review & send**), overdue invoices (**Open**) and clients with unbilled work (**Invoice** starts a new invoice for that client). **→** opens Reports.
- **Today**: the icon in the card's header is a small sky showing today's weather (sunny, cloudy, rain, snow, night and so on) for the business address in **Settings**. Click it and the sky opens across the header with the temperature, conditions, high and low, and chance of rain or snow; click again to go back. The pin button there switches to where you are (your browser asks once). Below, everything due today in one list. **Overdue** first (tasks past their due date and next steps that are late), then **Today**: bookings and your shifts today, tasks and next steps due today, form submissions waiting for review, and email to file. The check button marks a task or an account's next step done. Next steps more than 30 days late collapse into one line at the bottom; **Clean up** opens them in **Activity → To do → Next steps**.
- **My work** (roles with Tasks): tasks assigned to you. **New for you** lists tasks someone else gave you and tasks with a new comment since your last visit. **Due this week** lists the rest due in the next seven days, overdue first. Each row shows who gave you the task (their avatar), the project, subtasks done and the latest comment. The line at the bottom counts tasks due later or with no date; **All tasks** opens Tasks.
- **Pipeline & relationships**: open cards in your first pipeline, the total of their **Estimated value**, and each lane with its card count and a bar showing its share of the open cards. Click a lane to open Pipeline. **Gone quiet** lists active accounts nobody has contacted in a while, with days since last contact. **Next steps this week** lists next steps coming up. **→** opens Pipeline.
- **Since last time**: what happened in the last week, newest first: payments received, comments on your tasks, tasks given to you, bookings made, form submissions and email that came in unfiled. Anything after your last visit is tagged **New**. **→** opens Activity.
- **My time** (roles with Time): hours this week, the share that is billable, and a bar for each day Monday to Sunday. A running timer shows with a stop button; otherwise **Log time** opens Time.
- **My shifts** (Care module, roles with Schedule): your shifts for the next week. **Start visit** on today's shifts starts the visit for that shift; **Open visit** once it's started. **→** opens the Schedule.

## Rearranging

Drag a card by the **⋯** handle in its header to move it. Cards are full width (Financial health), half width (Today, My work) or a third of the row (the rest); the others fill any gaps. Your order is remembered on this computer and browser.
