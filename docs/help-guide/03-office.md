# Office (Clients, Projects, Tasks, Time, Expenses, Invoices)

## Clients

**Clients** lists the organizations you are working for (status Client). **Include past clients** adds past clients. See the CRM guide for how clients, prospects and organizations relate.

## Projects

**Projects** lists billable work tied to clients. **New Project** creates one. Open a project to edit details, contacts, and tasks.

On a project, **Contact people** are client-side contacts for invoicing and communication. Each contact is a record in **People** — use **New person** or **Add from People** to add them, then **Save** the project to refresh their name, email, and photo from People. Use **Open in People** on a contact row to edit their profile or photo. Older project-only contacts (without a People link) should be removed and re-added from People.

## Tasks

**Tasks** opens Task Manager: my tasks, inbox, and projects. Tasks-only users live here exclusively.

## Time

**Time** (Time entry) lists time entries. Add time from the list or related modals; tie entries to projects and tasks when applicable.

## Expenses

**Expenses** lists expenses. **New Expense** on the left; **Total** shows filtered total next to it; search on the far right.

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists invoices. **New Invoice** creates a draft. Open a row for detail; drafts open in edit mode. Overdue/due warnings may appear above the table.
