# Platform (super user only)

## Tenants

**Tenants** lists organizations on the platform. **Add tenant** creates one; **Switch into** loads that tenant’s data. Super users pick the active tenant from the top bar tenant dropdown when they have more than one.

## Guide setup

**Guide setup** configures the in-app **Back Office Guide**:

1. Choose **Provider** (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Gemini)
2. Enter exact **Model** ID (e.g. `claude-sonnet-4-5`)
3. Paste **API key** (required first time; leave blank on later saves to keep existing key)
4. **Save & test connection**

After configuration, all back-office users see the sparkle **Guide** avatar at the bottom-left of the screen.
